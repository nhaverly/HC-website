How savings are worked out

Owning the home

The council borrows the full cost from the PWLB on a maturity loan at the certainty rate and sets aside minimum revenue provision each year. It lets at the current LHA rate for the postcode and bedroom size, and pays management and maintenance as a share of that rent.

Which homes are shown

Every listing whose net saving is above zero, at any price and with any number of bedrooms. The area and energy rating choices narrow this down.

This is a screening figure. A full appraisal adds MRP, HRA or General Fund treatment, repairs and void profiles, rent growth against a frozen LHA and a sensitivity range on the nightly cost. The 2024-25 nightly averages are likely to understate current prices.

Where the data comes from

Listings: sample properties for demonstration, each at a real postcode. Asking prices, works costs, floor areas and EPC ratings are illustrative.

LHA rates: 2026/27 and January 2011 weekly rates for each postcode, from the Valuation Office Agency's LHA Direct.

Nightly TA costs: each London borough's 2024-25 revenue outturn (RO4) spend on nightly-paid self-contained TA divided by its average households in that category (TA1), as compiled by the London TA Atlas. Boroughs whose returns can't isolate nightly-paid costs use the London average of £94.

Subsidy rules: Local Government Association, the LHA rate and temporary accommodation reimbursement process.
