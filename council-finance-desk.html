Tools

Council finance questions, answered with the source.

Ask about local authority finance in England and get the rule, where it comes from, what it means in practice and a worked example where numbers matter.

What it covers

  • Governance and budgetss151, s114 and s25, the FM Code, reserves and the MTFS
  • FundingThe 2026/27 multi-year settlement, council tax limits, business rates and Exceptional Financial Support
  • Capital and treasuryThe Prudential Code, MRP, capital receipts flexibility, PWLB and treasury management
  • Accounts and auditThe 2026/27 Code, IFRS 16, statutory overrides, backstops and the Local Audit Office
  • AlsoDSG, temporary accommodation, VAT, the Procurement Act, LGPS, fraud and reorganisation
  • How to use itRules checked to 13 November 2026. CIPFA codes are explained in plain English, not quoted. For detailed HRA questions, use the HRA Assistant.
